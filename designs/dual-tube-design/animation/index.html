A simultaneous release, shown at real time and replayed at quarter speed. Sounds and visible labels mark each landing. Predicted arrivals: magnet 2.738 s; control 0.249 s.

About the simulation

Eddy currents in the copper tube slow the magnet. The cutaway reveals its motion; the modeled copper tube wall remains electrically intact.

This fixed configuration simulation predicts the falls from nominal material properties and the specified geometry.
