About the simulation

Both paths contain a copper tube inside a clear sleeve. Eddy currents in the copper slow the magnetic ring; the nonmagnetic control follows free fall.

The prototype uses a ¾″ OD × ½″ ID × ⅜″ high ring around 12 mm OD × 1 mm wall copper, with 12″ tubes. The model includes finite tube ends and integrates braking throughout the copper wall.

These are predictions for centered motion with nominal material properties and no rubbing. The timing uses stop planes inferred from the end-cap mesh; actual cap clearances and measured magnet strength can change the result.
