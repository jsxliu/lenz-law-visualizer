# See-through animation

[← See-through design and build guide](https://github.com/jsxliu/lenz-law-visualizer/tree/main/designs/see-through-design)

**[Watch the animation (12.05 seconds)](https://jsxliu.github.io/lenz-law-visualizer/designs/see-through-design/animation/)**

The player includes a **Download video (MP4)** link.

A ring magnet falls **around the outside of a copper tube**, beside a matching
nonmagnetic control ring. Both paths have the same copper tube and clear outer
sleeve. The simultaneous release is shown at real time and replayed at quarter
speed. Predicted arrivals are **1.004 s** for the magnet and **0.237 s** for the
free-fall control. Landing sounds are illustrative contact cues.

The apparatus view shows black upper and lower caps, a yellow control ring,
and dimensions in inches and millimeters.

This fixed simulation uses the prototype's **¾″ OD × ½″ ID × ⅜″ high ring**,
**12 mm OD × 1 mm wall copper**, and **12″ tube length**. The simulation adapts
the cylindrical-magnet field from the
[Derby–Olbert reproduction](https://github.com/jsxliu/derby-olbert-2010)
to the ring geometry and calculates its motion, including finite tube ends
and copper wall thickness. The control follows analytic free fall.
